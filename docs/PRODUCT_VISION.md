# Product vision

ONECRM is an AI-native, all-in-one business operating system for India and global markets: one platform where a business manages customers, sales, communication, marketing, support, finance workflows, work, automation and intelligence — configurable to many industries, not a separate product per industry.

**Goal:** reduce repetitive work, duplicate data entry, app switching, missed follow-ups and delayed decisions, while keeping people in control of important decisions.

## Principles (non-negotiable)
1. **One unified platform** — shared identity, customer records, permissions, search, notifications, history and automation infrastructure; no duplicated customer data.
2. **Simplicity despite breadth** — progressive disclosure, role-specific workspaces, search-first navigation, smart defaults.
3. **AI-native** — AI inside real workflows (summaries, prioritisation, drafting, permitted actions), never a bolted-on chatbot or a fake effect.
4. **Automation-first** — repetitive work automated wherever safe.
5. **Human control** — AI never bypasses authorization; sensitive actions need approval.
6. **Enterprise reliability** — security, isolation, audit, backup, observability, performance are foundations.
7. **Universal foundation** — new modules/industries without rebuilding the core.
8. **Measurable value** — each major feature has a business purpose and success criteria.

## Where we are
Phase 1 (identity, tenancy, roles, CRM core, tasks, timeline, audit) is built and tested; the AI, automation, communication and business-operations layers are designed but **not built**. This repository does not yet deliver the "intelligently prepare and automate work" end state — it delivers the secure, connected data foundation that state requires.

## Success looks like
A new business onboards without technical help; employees see their day immediately; customer information is connected; salespeople do less admin; managers see the business without assembling reports; workflows run reliably; AI acts only within strict permissions; enterprises can trust its security and audit trail. Each is tracked in `IMPLEMENTATION_STATUS.md` against evidence, not intent.
